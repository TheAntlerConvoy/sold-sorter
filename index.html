Sort eBay results by number sold

Sold Sorter is a browser extension for Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge. It adds a "Sort by most sold" button to eBay search and category pages and shows how many of each item have sold.

Add to Chrome Free, with Pro for more. Coming soon to Microsoft Edge.

eBay's search page often shows a lower number like "10,000+ sold", or no number at all. Sold Sorter reads the full total from each listing page.

Features

Free

  • Sort eBay search results and category pages by number sold
  • A sold badge on every listing
  • Works on eBay in the US, UK, Germany, Australia, Canada, France, Italy, Spain, Austria, Switzerland, Ireland, the Netherlands, Belgium, Poland, Singapore, Malaysia and Hong Kong

Pro

  • Sort up to 10 result pages together
  • Estimated profit after eBay fees and shipping on every listing
  • Export results to a CSV file for Excel or Google Sheets
